# Murmelbahn

A wooden marble run on a pegboard wall. Glass marbles with different colours and slightly different weights drop from a dispenser, roll along rails, and ring whatever they hit.

**Play:** open `index.html` (one file, no network, works on a phone).

## How to play
- **Bauen** places parts on the grid. Tap an empty peg to set the selected part, tap a part again to select it.
- **Drehen** turns the selection (or the part you are about to place). **Grösse** changes a bell or a xylophone bar: a bigger one sounds lower.
- **Spielen** lets the marbles go. **Takt** chooses how often the dispenser lets one go.
- **Rückgängig**, **Leeren**, and three **Beispiele**: Bach (a quiet staircase), Melodie (the bars play a little tune), Uhrwerk (lift wheel, spiral, splitter, seesaw, trampoline).
- **Teilen** copies a link. The layout is in the address after the `#`, and it is also kept on this device.
- **Ton** stays off until you tap it. Then you get wooden clacks, a soft roll, bells, and the bars.

Labels are Swiss German (ss, never ß). Drag the board when it does not fit, which is mostly landscape phones.

## Parts
Straight and curved rails, a funnel, a spiral, a seesaw, a bell, a xylophone bar, a lift wheel that carries marbles back up, a splitter that alternates left and right, a trampoline, and a catch basket.

Marbles collide with each other and with the rails (a little bounce, then they roll). Up to about 40 are on the board at once; the step size stays fixed so a busy moment does not skip through a rail.
