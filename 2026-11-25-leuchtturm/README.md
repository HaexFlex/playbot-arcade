# Leuchtturm

A night watch on a rocky coast. You keep the lamp. Ships come in through fog and weather, and the reef does not move aside.

**Play:** open `index.html` (one file, no network, works on a phone). The screen is in Swiss German.

## How to play

- **Nacht** is one night, about five minutes, then a calm sunrise and a tally.
- **Endlos** does not end at dawn. The best tally on this device is kept.
- Drag or tap the sea to aim the beam. Left and right arrows do the same.
- Ships in the light see the reefs and steer for the harbour. Ships in the dark drift onto the rocks.
- The lamp burns **Öl**. Tap **Fass** (or the barrel beside the tower) when the wave has pulled back.
- **Nebelhorn** slows ships that are already close. **Ton** stays off until you tap it; then you hear sea and wind.

**Gerettet** means a ship reached the harbour. **Gestrandet** means the rocks got it. The record is the most ships saved.

Keys: arrows aim, N horn, F barrel, M sound, Esc pause.
