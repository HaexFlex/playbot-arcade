# Kite Day

A quiet afternoon on a windy hill. The kite is already up. Hold to pull the string in, let go to pay it out, and watch it climb, sag, and wander as the wind gusts.

Open `index.html`. One file, no installs, fine on a phone or a computer. Sound stays off until you tap **Sound**.

## How to play

- Hold on the sky (or press Space or the down arrow) to reel in. Let go and the line runs out again.
- The string is a rope: it goes tight when the kite pulls, and it sags when you give it slack. The tail ripples behind.
- Wind comes in gusts. The grass leans, the clouds hurry, leaves blow past, and the striped wind sock lifts.
- Fly through the rings, the little birds, and the balloons. A firm pull and a release, while you are high, sends the kite through a loop.
- Your best height is kept on this device.

## Kites

Diamond, Delta, Box, Dragon, Butterfly, and Swallow. Each one flies a little differently. The dragon's tail is the long one.

## The day

Morning slides into afternoon, sunset, dusk, and a starry night, then back again. A hawk may circle up beside you. At dusk a paper lantern lifts off the hill. Now and then the tail snags a balloon and carries it for a moment.

## Sound

Tap **Sound** for a soft wind (it swells with the gusts), a hum that rises as the string pulls tight, and a small chime when you fly through a ring. It is all made in the browser. Nothing plays until that tap.
