# Seifenblasen

A sunny garden for blowing soap bubbles. Hold the ring still and a big wobbly bubble grows on it. Pull the wand quickly and a stream of small ones peels off. They drift on the breeze, wobble, and stick into soft clusters.

**Play:** open `index.html` (one file, no network, works on a phone). The page speaks Swiss German (de-CH, so "ss" and never "ß").

## How to play

- **Halten** or move slowly: a bubble inflates on the ring. Let go and it floats off. The slower and longer you hold, the bigger and wobblier it is.
- **Schnell ziehen**: the ring sheds a stream of small bubbles along the stroke.
- **Wind** is the breeze. It shows in the grass, the clouds and the way the bubbles drift.
- **Seifenmischung** is how long the film lasts: kurz, mittel, lang or haltbar. The colours swirl and the film thins until it goes dark, just before the pop.
- **Schaumbad** drops a little foam cluster. Bubbles that meet gently stick. Their shared walls sit near a 120° junction, the way a real soap foam does.
- **Rekord** is the longest a bubble has lived on this device (`localStorage`). **Älteste** is the one still in the air.
- The dog likes low bubbles. It runs over and jumps for them. A catch pops the bubble into droplets.
- **Ton** is off until you turn it on, and only after a tap. Then blowing is a soft rush of air and each pop is a little blip, made in the browser. Big bubbles pop lower.

Keys: M sound, S or B for a foam cluster.
