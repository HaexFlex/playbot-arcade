# Modelleisenbahn

A model railway on a wooden table, seen from above with a slight tilt. Lay track, throw the switches, and let the trains run.

**Play:** open `index.html` (one file, no network, works on a phone). The page speaks German (de-CH, so "ss" and never "ß").

## How to play
- **Zeiger** drags the table. Tap a **Weiche** to throw it. Pinch or the mouse wheel zooms. **+**, **−** and **Passend** do the same.
- **Gerade**, **Kurve**, **Weiche**, **Kreuzung**, **Prellbock**, **Bahnhof**, **Tunnel**, **Brücke**. **Drehen** turns the piece, then drag to lay it.
- **Regional**, **Dampf** and **Güter** set a train on a track. Trains follow the rails, ease through curves, and stop at a Bahnhof so people can get on and off.
- Block signals stay green while the next stretch is free and turn red when a train is in it. A following train waits.
- **Haus**, **Baum**, **Kuh** and **See** dress the village. **Weg** lifts whatever is on a square. **Halt** pauses the clock.
- **Beispiele:** Kreis, Achterbahn, Bergstrecke.
- **Teilen** writes the layout into the address hash. The table is also kept on this device.
- **Ton** stays off until you tap it. After that the rails click, the steam engine chuffs, and a station gives a small chime. Nothing plays before the first tap.
- **Tag**, **Nacht** and **Verlauf**. House and carriage windows light up after dark.

**Fahrgäste** counts people who boarded. **Pünktlichkeit** is the share of station stops that were not held long at a red signal.

Keys: 1–9 pick a tool, R rotate, space pause, M sound, F fit the table, Esc back to the pointer.
