# Eisbahn

A village pond at dusk, frozen over. Lanterns along the bank, snow in the air, and skate tracks that slowly soften back into the ice.

**Play:** open `index.html` (one file, no network, works on a phone). The page speaks Swiss German (de-CH): ss, and no sharp s.

## How to play
- **Ziehen** on the ice. The front skater (gold ring) glides along that line and carves a trail. A closed loop is skated again and again. An open line is skated once.
- **Läufer** sends another skater onto their own oval. **Weniger** takes the last one off. Up to six share the pond.
- **Pirouette** spins the front skater on the spot. **Achter** sends them through one figure eight, then they rest.
- **Eismaschine** drives a wide lap across the pond and smooths the scratches away. The pale stripe is the wet ice it leaves behind.
- **Ton** stays off until you tap it. Then the blades scratch with speed, the machine hums while it works, and a small bell marks a figure.

Keys, when focus is not in a field: P pirouette, A figure eight, L another skater, W one fewer, E the machine, M sound.

Trails fade on their own. Snow keeps falling. Nothing is stored.
