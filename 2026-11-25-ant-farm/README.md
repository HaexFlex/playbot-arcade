# Ameisenfarm

A glass ant farm on a wooden desk. Under a strip of meadow, the soil is a real grid: workers dig it out, carry the crumbs up, and leave tunnels and chambers behind.

**Play:** open `index.html` (one file, no network, works on a phone). The page is in German (de-CH).

## How to play
- **Zucker**, **Samen** or **Apfel**, then tap the meadow or an open tunnel to leave food.
- **Wasser** drips into the glass. Rain can pool a little in the upper tunnels.
- **Stein** sets a pebble the ants will not dig through.
- **Licht** is the default: tap to shine a small torch.
- **Klopf** (or tap the wooden frame) makes the colony scurry.
- **Spuren** shows the pheromone trails.
- **Notiz** is the colony notebook: ants, tunnels dug, food gathered, days.
- **Ton** stays off until you tap it. Then: soft digging and rustle, meadow by day, crickets at night.

The queen lays eggs. Eggs become larvae, then pupae, then workers. Workers dig, forage, follow trails, stock a pantry, and tend the brood. The farm is saved on this device.

Keys: M sound, 1 sugar, 2 seed, 3 apple, 4 water, 5 pebble, L light, K knock, P trails, N notebook, Esc closes the notebook.
