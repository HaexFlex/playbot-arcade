# Evening Tea Room: plan

A night counter that can keep growing. One self-contained `index.html`, Swiss German in the page, English through the shared `playbot-lang` switch.

## Done

- Kettle: heats, sings, cools when taken off the flame, boils dry, refills cold
- Pot: one tea at a time, steep rate depends on heat, strainer, bitter mark, second steep on used leaves
- Cup cools on the counter. Honey, lemon, milk, and mint are chosen before the pour. Milk curdles in green, floral, and mint teas
- Six regulars with different heats, strengths, and additions. A candle shows patience. They leave kindly, and the guestbook can invite them back
- Weather cycle (rain, clear, snow, wind) changes the window, the sound, the candle, and the cup they enjoy
- Guestbook of what they let slip, recipe cards for delighted cups, six window stars
- New tins as gifts: ginger, green, jasmine, rooibos, elderflower, and bergamot from the cat Semmel
- Saved on this device (`teestube-abend-v1`)
- Web Audio after the first gesture: rain, hiss, whistle, pour, clink, bell, purr, a small chime. Mute button
- Desktop, portrait phone, and short landscape. Touch and a few keys
- Coach glow on the first cup only
- Sill mint: snip a sprig (stronger than the tin), it droops while the kettle stays screaming, and a tired sprig does not satisfy a guest who wanted mint. Leaves grow back once it is cool again
- Guestbook chapters: each delight adds a line. Lio's chapter, Herr Quell's postcard, and a short arc for every regular, kept in the old save
- The night moves on its own clock: evening, late (the rain thins), toward dawn (a clear starry sky). A new evening brings the rain back without wiping the book
- Ivo, from the night shift, only joins the queue once it is late
- Closing up, from the button or the door, sends the room to dawn. Opening again keeps stars, tins, notes, and the mint
