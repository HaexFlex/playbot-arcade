How to play

  • Pick a path up the map. Each act ends with a boss. Beat three bosses to win.
  • In combat you get 3 energy and draw 5 cards each turn. Cards cost energy (top-left number).
  • Glow blocks damage until your next turn. Enemies show their intent above their heads: plan around it.
  • Dazzled: deals 25% less damage. Webbed: takes 50% more. Kindled: burns each turn. Damp: less Glow.
  • Win fights to add cards. Rest or upgrade at campfires. Elites drop charms.
  • Dew vials (top bar, 3 slots) are free to use during your turn. Fights sometimes drop them; the Moth Trader sells them. Click a vial outside combat twice to toss it.
  • Swarm (Moth Whisperer): at the end of your turn, your moths deal damage equal to your Swarm to a random enemy.
  • Keys: Z X C use a vial, 1-9 pick a card, ← → + Enter choose target, E end turn, D deck, M mute, Esc cancel/menu.
  • Touch: tap a card, then tap an enemy (or tap the card again).
