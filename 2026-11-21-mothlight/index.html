Mothlight

How to play

  • Each turn you either switch one lantern on or off (tap it), or wait. Then every moth flies one step.
  • A moth flies toward the nearest lit lantern (counted in straight grid steps). It closes the longer gap first; on equal gaps it goes sideways first.
  • If a tree or lantern is in the way, it tries its other direction; if that is blocked too, it hovers.
  • Two lit lanterns at the same distance confuse a moth: it stays where it is. In the dark, moths rest.
  • Lead every moth into a jar. Jars hold any number of moths. Moths that touch a spider web are stuck, so undo.
  • Faint arrows preview where each moth will go. Hover a lantern to preview switching it.
